Privacy policy

Updated October 24, 2026

Fizzgig Apps publishes Belay Buddy and EDC Roulette. This page covers what is shared between them: the sign-in and the account. Each app also has its own privacy policy, which describes what that app does with your information and is the one that applies to the app's own features:

  • Belay Buddy privacy policy
  • EDC Roulette privacy policy

Signing in

An app may let you sign in with Google, Apple, Facebook or an email address. When you do, we receive from that provider your email address (Apple may give a private relay address instead) and, where the provider shares them, your name and profile picture. We use these to create your account, recognise you when you come back, and show your name in the app. We do not receive your provider password.

Signing in with Google uses Google's standard sign-in, limited to your email address and basic profile. Fizzgig Apps' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

One account, separate data

The same sign-in can be used in more than one of our apps. Your account details (the information above) are shared by those apps. Each app keeps its own data in its own separate area, and the data of one app is not shown inside another.

Where it is stored, and who else handles it

Account and app data is stored with our database host, Supabase. Sign-in providers (Google, Apple, Facebook) handle your sign-in under their own privacy policies. Each app's policy lists any other services that app uses.

What we don't do

We do not sell your information, and we do not use account details for advertising.

Your choices

You can delete your account and its data from the app's settings where the app offers it, or by emailing us from the address on the account with the subject "Delete my account". We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

Children

Our apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: support@belaybuddy.app or support@edcroulette.app.
